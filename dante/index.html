Dante® flow & bandwidth planner

List the devices and who sends audio to whom. The planner counts the flows each device needs, estimates the load on every link and writes a one-page brief for the IT team.

Multicast ch: how many of this device's channels it sends as multicast (one stream any number of receivers can pick up). Leave at 0 for ordinary one-to-one audio. Switch: type the same name for devices plugged into the same switch.

3Audio routes

FromToChannelsSent asFlows

A route means every unit on the From line sends that many channels to every unit on the To line. So "DSP to Amplifiers (quantity 4), 2 channels" is 2 channels to each of the 4 amplifiers.
